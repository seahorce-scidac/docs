Detailed Description

Common host-side ecosystem interface.

Configure once before querying the declaration and allocating scalar state. Concrete models own parameters, option validation, and kernels. Analytic initial profiles belong to the problem (ProblemBase::init_analytic_biology). Runtime selection is confined to make_biology; callers do not downcast. No additional clipping or mathematical constraints belong in this interface.
