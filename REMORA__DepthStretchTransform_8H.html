MFItInfo RowTilingIfNotGPU ( )
inline

Tiling for the loops of this file: boxes are split only in j (and k), so every tile spans the full i extent of its box.

A compiler that contracts multiply-adds (GCC with -march=native, the Cray wrappers on Perlmutter) may round the same expression differently in the vectorized body of the i loop and in its remainder. With tiles that split i, which cells fall in the remainder depends on the tile size, and z_w, z_r and Hz then changed in their last bits with remora.omp_tile_size (8x8 tiles differed from 16-row strips and from no tiling; the differences grew to 1e-8 in the velocities within five steps). With full-row tiles every cell has the same position in the i loop whatever the tiling. Untiled (GPU) iteration is unchanged.
