Detailed Description

The two components of a vector read from NetCDF files, such as Uwind and Vwind or sustr and svstr.

On the model grid a file gives the components along the grid's axes, at the points of the model targets: rho points for the winds, u and v points for the stress. On a lat-lon grid it gives them east and north. Both are then interpolated to rho points, into the rho targets, and the caller turns them onto the grid's axes there and, for the stress, averages them to the faces (REMORA::rho_vector_to_grid_axes). A component given as one value per time pairs with either kind of grid.

Collective: construct, initialize and update on every rank.
