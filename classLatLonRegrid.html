Detailed Description

Bilinear interpolation of a 2D field from a rectilinear lat-lon grid onto REMORA's rho points, the way ROMS's regrid does for forcing.

The source grid is given by 1D longitude and latitude axes, each strictly monotone in either direction; the longitude may be in [-180, 180) or [0, 360), may cross the branch cut, and may cover the globe, in which case it is treated as periodic. The target points are the model's lon_rho and lat_rho on level 0 over the domain and its one-cell ring, the cells a file on the model grid fills.

The interpolation weights are computed once, in define(), and stored per target cell in the layout the coupling remap uses: up to four weights, and the (i, j, k) source index of each. apply() reads one record of the field, sets aside source values equal to the variable's _FillValue or missing_value (or not finite), unpacks the rest with its scale_factor and add_offset, renormalizes the remaining weights, and gives a target point whose four corners are all missing the nearest valid source value within LatLonRegridOptions::fill_max_rings cells.
