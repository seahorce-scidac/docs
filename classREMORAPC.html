Uses midpoint method to advance particles using flow velocity.

Midpoint-method advection in terrain-following coordinates. Horizontally the particle moves with u and v at a fixed fractional w-level index (see REMORA_PC.H), so it follows the s-levels over sloping bathymetry; vertically it moves across the levels at omega / Hz. The index is reflected off the bed and the free surface and then mapped back to a height, so a particle can never leave the water column.

Particle positions are in the uniform coordinates of the AMReX geometry, whose cell size is (prob_hi - prob_lo) / n_cell, while u and v are physical velocities along the grid directions. They are converted with the local metric, dx/dt = u * pm * dx_geom and dy/dt = v * pn * dy_geom, so particles cover the right physical distance on stretched or curvilinear grids; on a uniform grid the factors are 1.

The index is recovered from the stored height on the current grid, so a particle stays at a fixed height, not a fixed index, while the free surface moves between steps.

Write the particles to a plotfile with x and y in physical coordinates

Write the particles to a plotfile with x and y in physical coordinates. The particles move in the uniform coordinates of the AMReX geometry; for output, x and y are replaced by the physical coordinates of the cell corners (psi points) interpolated bilinearly to each particle. These are the corner positions the plotfile's nodal displacement gives a viewer, so each particle is drawn inside the cell that holds it. z is already the physical height. Only a copy of the particles is converted, so writing a plotfile does not change the run.

Parameters
a_fnameplotfile directory
a_nameparticle species name
a_xyper level, physical x and y of the psi points (REMORA::plot_node_coords)
