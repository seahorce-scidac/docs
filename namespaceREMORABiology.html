◆ CO2SchmidtType

enum class REMORABiology::CO2SchmidtType
strong

Schmidt-number formulation for the air-sea gas transfer velocity.

CO2 offers the Wanninkhof (1992) and (2014) coefficient sets; O2 adds the Keeling et al. (1998) OCMIP set. The choice also selects the leading rate coefficient, 0.31 for 1992 and 0.251 for 2014, so the two must move together and are not independently settable.

Source of the atmospheric pCO2 used by the surface CO2 gas exchange.

Vertical sinking of one particulate tracer in column (i,j), by the ROMS piecewise-parabolic reconstruction with WENO-reconciled interfaces and monotone boundary closures, followed by a CFL-free semi-Lagrangian flux. Shared by the Fennel and ECB kernels, whose ROMS sources carry identical copies.

nlev is the number of layers, k = 0..nlev-1. On entry qc holds the concentration to sink (each model restricts it as its ROMS source does). On exit bio holds the sunk concentration and FC(i,j,0) the flux through the bottom face, which the caller returns at the seafloor. sink_distance is dtdays * |w|. qc, bR, bL, WR and WL are cell scratch, and FC face scratch, over at least the column.

Air-sea exchange factors at rho point (i,j) from the temperature [degC] and salinity of surface layer ksurf and the wind or stress.

They depend only on those, so a kernel computes them once per column, not once per BioIter iteration. The O2 factors are computed only with oxygen and the CO2 ones only with carbon; the others stay zero. With neither, the salinity, wind and stress views are not read, so they may be empty.
