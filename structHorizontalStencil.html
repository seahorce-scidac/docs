Detailed Description

Lower-left cell (i, j) and fractional offsets (fx, fy) of the bilinear stencil at horizontal position (x, y), for data nodal or cell-centered in each of x and y (nodal data at (i,j) sits at the low corner of cell (i,j)). Every horizontal interpolation of grid data to a particle goes through this, so the staggering and the half-cell offset are defined in one place.
