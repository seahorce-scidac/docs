Locate height a_z in the column at (a_x, a_y). A height below the bed or above the free surface is clamped onto it. a_k, the cell the particle was last in, is where the search starts. A cell of zero (or negative) thickness, as in a dry or degenerate column, gives f = 0 rather than a division by zero.

Trilinear interpolation of a_arr to horizontal position (a_x, a_y) and fractional w-level index a_s. a_nodal gives a_arr's staggering. Data that is cell-centered in z is held constant over the half cells at the bed and the surface, so the vertical stencil stays within the valid cells [0,nz) (or w-levels [0,nz] for z-nodal data).
