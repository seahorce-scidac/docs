Fractional w-level index of height a_z in the column at (a_x, a_y). A height below the bed or above the free surface is clamped onto it. a_k, the cell the particle was last in, is where the search starts. If a_kcell is given, the cell the search ended in and the thickness of that cell at (a_x, a_y) are returned through a_kcell and a_dz, so a caller need not recompute the level heights.

Trilinear interpolation of a_arr to horizontal position (a_x, a_y) and fractional w-level index a_s. a_nodal gives a_arr's staggering. Data that is cell-centered in z is held constant over the half cells at the bed and the surface, so the vertical stencil stays within the valid cells [0,nz) (or w-levels [0,nz] for z-nodal data).
