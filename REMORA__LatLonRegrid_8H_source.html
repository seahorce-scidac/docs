35/**
36 * \brief Bilinear interpolation of a 2D field from a rectilinear lat-lon grid onto
37 * REMORA's rho points, the way ROMS's regrid does for forcing.
38 *
39 * The source grid is given by 1D longitude and latitude axes, each strictly monotone in
40 * either direction; the longitude may be in [-180, 180) or [0, 360), may cross the branch
41 * cut, and may cover the globe, in which case it is treated as periodic. The target points
42 * are the model's lon_rho and lat_rho on level 0 over the domain and its one-cell ring, the
43 * cells a file on the model grid fills.
44 *
45 * The interpolation weights are computed once, in define(), and stored per target cell in
46 * the layout the coupling remap uses: up to four weights, and the (i, j, k) source index of
47 * each. They depend only on the source axes and the target points, not on the variable, so
48 * every field on the same source grid and forcing layout shares one copy: define() hands out
49 * the weights an earlier field built, when there is one still in use. It also finds the
50 * box of source cells this rank's stencils and nearest-valid search can reach (across the
51 * seam of a periodic longitude, when that is shorter), and apply() reads only that box of
52 * each record, every rank at once in a collective read. apply() sets aside source values
53 * equal to the variable's _FillValue or missing_value (or not finite), unpacks the rest
54 * with its scale_factor and add_offset, renormalizes the remaining weights, and gives a
55 * target point whose four corners are all missing the nearest valid source value within
56 * LatLonRegridOptions::fill_max_rings cells.
57 */
