34/**
35 * \brief Bilinear interpolation of a 2D field from a rectilinear lat-lon grid onto
36 * REMORA's rho points, the way ROMS's regrid does for forcing.
37 *
38 * The source grid is given by 1D longitude and latitude axes, each strictly monotone in
39 * either direction; the longitude may be in [-180, 180) or [0, 360), may cross the branch
40 * cut, and may cover the globe, in which case it is treated as periodic. The target points
41 * are the model's lon_rho and lat_rho on level 0 over the domain and its one-cell ring, the
42 * cells a file on the model grid fills.
43 *
44 * The interpolation weights are computed once, in define(), and stored per target cell in
45 * the layout the coupling remap uses: up to four weights, and the (i, j, k) source index of
46 * each. apply() reads one record of the field, sets aside source values equal to the
47 * variable's _FillValue or missing_value (or not finite), unpacks the rest with its
48 * scale_factor and add_offset, renormalizes the remaining weights, and gives a target point
49 * whose four corners are all missing the nearest valid source value within
50 * LatLonRegridOptions::fill_max_rings cells.
51 */
