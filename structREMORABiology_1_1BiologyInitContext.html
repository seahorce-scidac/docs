Detailed Description

What ProblemBase::init_analytic_biology receives.

Analytic profiles are problem-specific and live with the problem in Exec/REMORA_Prob.cpp, not in the ecosystem model; they locate their fields with BiologyDeclaration::component_index. Physical fields have already been initialized. Only the declared biology components, starting at component_start, may be changed by the profile.
