14/** \brief The two components of a vector read from NetCDF files, such as Uwind and Vwind or
15 * sustr and svstr
16 *

18 * model targets: rho points for the winds, u and v points for the stress. On a lat-lon grid it
19 * gives them east and north. Both are then interpolated to rho points, into the rho targets,
20 * and the caller turns them onto the grid's axes there and, for the stress, averages them to
21 * the faces (REMORA::rho_vector_to_grid_axes). A component given as one value per time pairs
22 * with either kind of grid.
23 *
24 * Collective: construct, initialize and update on every rank.
25 */
